PAYMENT FLOW:
POST /api/v1/payments/create-order
POST /api/v1/payments/verify

Payment order creation now creates a PaymentTransaction with status "created".
Successful Razorpay verification changes it to "paid" and creates an Invoice.
Signature verification failure marks the tracked transaction as "failed".
